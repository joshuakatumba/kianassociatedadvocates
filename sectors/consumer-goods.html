Consumer Goods

Growth in the global consumer goods sector over the last decade has been strong. In addition to significant investment and innovation from market leaders in the sector, this has been driven by rising population numbers and incomes, particularly in emerging markets in Africa and Asia.

In Africa, growth in population, middle-class entrants, urbanisation and GDP per capita are all forecast to exceed growth in developed markets for the foreseeable future. This will continue to create significant opportunities for participants in the consumer goods sector.

At the same time, companies continue to face a number of significant challenges to traditional operating models. Among these challenges are heavier taxes and increasing (often uncertain) regulation. The influence of technology, especially developments in online retail and social media, on traditional retail and consumer-focused businesses also cannot be overlooked.

We are uniquely positioned to advise companies on all aspects of their businesses in Africa.

Our extensive experience in the consumer goods sector allows us to provide comprehensive legal and strategic advice to our clients. We have successfully represented multinational corporations, local enterprises, and innovative startups, helping them navigate complex regulatory landscapes and execute critical business transactions.

We understand the unique commercial drivers and specific risks associated with this industry, enabling us to deliver practical, commercially viable solutions tailored to our clients' strategic objectives. From joint ventures and acquisitions to regulatory compliance and dispute resolution, our team offers unparalleled expertise and dedication.
